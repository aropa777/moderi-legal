more.jump Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

more.jump (the "App") does not collect any personal data.

1. Data We Collect

None. The App does not collect or transmit names, email addresses, location, usage history, device identifiers, or any other personal information. It contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your registered slots (apps and folders), site list, shortcuts, and preferences are stored only on your device. If iCloud sync is used, these settings are stored in your own iCloud account via Apple's iCloud key-value store; the developer has no access to them.

3. Safari Automation

The site-jump feature uses macOS Automation (Apple Events) solely to switch to or open Safari tabs. Tab addresses are compared on-device only for matching; no browsing data is stored or transmitted. This permission is optional and can be revoked at any time in macOS System Settings.

4. Payments

Pro purchases are processed by the Apple App Store; the developer never has access to your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles payment data.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy: aropa777@gmail.com

6. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page, and material changes will be noted in App update notes.
