Helper – Auto Scroll

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026

Auto Scroll does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Everything happens on your device, inside Safari.

Information we do not collect

We collect no information of any kind — no name, email, device identifiers, location, website addresses you visit, or web page content. There is no account or sign-in.

Web page access by the Safari extension

When you turn on the extension, Safari says it can read and alter web pages. Auto Scroll uses this access only to:

  • scroll the page automatically and show its control orb at the bottom right of the screen
  • check whether the middle of the screen is empty so it can pass blank areas faster (checked momentarily on your device and never stored)

Page content is never saved or sent off your device. Auto Scroll has no feature that communicates with any server.

Information stored on your device

Only settings such as on/off, scroll speed, and orb position are kept in Safari's extension storage on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Analytics and advertising

We use no analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or advertising.

Children's privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the effective date and what changed on this page and mention it in the app's update notes.
