Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a folder?

Drag and drop any folder onto the drop zone, or click the folder icon in the header to select one.

Is the Purge feature permanent?

No. Files are moved to the Trash, not permanently deleted. You can restore them from Trash or use the Undo feature in more.scan.

How do I restore purged files?

After purging, tap "되돌리기 (Undo)" on the completion screen to restore files to their original location.

What is more.scan Pro?

more.scan Pro unlocks the Purge feature for a one-time purchase of $6.99.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open more.scan, click the Purge button, and tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen.
