Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

more.scan does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All file scanning and analysis happens entirely on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

more.scan does not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not access, read, upload, or share your files or file contents with any third party.

File Access

more.scan requires access to folders you explicitly select. This access is used solely to scan and display file information within the app. No file data is transmitted off your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Analytics

more.scan does not use any analytics, tracking, or crash reporting services. No usage data is collected.

Children's Privacy

more.scan does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
