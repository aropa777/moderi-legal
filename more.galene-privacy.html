Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

more.galene does not access your camera or microphone, and does not require an account or login. Almost all data stays on your device.

Information We Don't Collect

more.galene does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address. There is no account or login system, and the app does not access your camera, microphone, or other sensors.

Information Stored On Your Device

Your credit balance, selected sounds, mixer configuration, and language preference are stored locally on your device only. This information is never transmitted to any server, and is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

more.galene shows rewarded video ads only on the "Credit Store" screen. No ads appear on the home screen or the player screen.

Ads are currently served on a non-personalized basis. Google and its advertising partners may collect a limited set of device identifiers for the purposes of serving ads and preventing fraud, in accordance with Google's policies. We do not directly access or store this data.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

more.galene currently has no in-app purchases. If a subscription or other paid feature is introduced in the future, all payments will be processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, and we will not directly collect or store payment information. This policy will be updated if that changes.

Analytics & Tracking

more.galene does not currently use any analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Health-Related Notice

more.galene is a wellness application built around white noise and ambient sound. It is not a medical device, and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition. If you experience persistent sleep or focus difficulties, please consult a qualified professional.

Baby Safety Notice

The "Sleep" mode is a supplemental white-noise feature for nighttime use, and is not a substitute for a baby monitor. Volume limits and other safety-related features in the app are software-based limits only, and do not guarantee safety. Always follow the in-app safety guidance (such as keeping the device at least 2 meters from the crib) and use the app under direct adult supervision.

Children's Privacy

more.galene is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is listed under a general (adult) audience rating on app stores.

Your Rights

Because more.galene collects very limited personal information, there is little data to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about your data, feel free to contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
